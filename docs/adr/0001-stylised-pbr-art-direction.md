# Stylised PBR, not v2's pixel art

v2 is pixel art, and the obvious 3D translation is low-poly geometry with nearest-filtered pixel textures. We chose stylised PBR instead: `MeshStandardMaterial` on chunky, exaggerated forms, with materials generated by bpy scripts and baked to texture sets. The palette survives as an **authoring** palette. Every material input, light colour and emissive comes from `P` / `LIGHT` in one `src/render/palette.json`, read by both TypeScript and the Blender scripts. Lit, tone-mapped pixels may leave the palette. Readability comes from v2's hierarchy and colour reservations (fire for hazards, gold for pickups, teal for mummies, steel for blades and spikes), not from a fixed set of output colours.

## Considered options

- **Low-poly + palette pixel textures**: the closest to v2's identity, and agents could port v2's `paint*` routines. Passed over for the richer stylised-PBR look.
- **Flat-shaded low-poly**: the cheapest, but looks like a greybox unless the geometry carries the detail.
- **Hand-painted textures**: every surface needs human painting time.
- **Grounded-realism PBR / CC0 colour maps**: detail can't be seen from the follow camera (about 120 px per tile), and scanned colours bypass the palette. CC0 maps are allowed for greyscale detail only (normal, roughness, AO).
- **Strict output palette (toon quantisation)**: shader-heavy, and it fights PBR shading.

## Consequences

- v2's ramps become **surface variation** (block tone, mortar, wear), not painted lighting. Real lights do the top/side/shadow shading.
- PBR metals use the light end of their ramp as base colour (`STEEL_LIGHT`, not `STEEL`), because a metal's base colour is its reflectance.
- Tone mapping is `NeutralToneMapping`, not ACES, so the reserved signal colours keep their hue on screen.
- Metals need an environment map, so every chamber gets a palette gradient environment map (PMREM).
- Bloom is the only bright-glow mechanism: only emissive at intensity ≥ 2 crosses the threshold. Pickups glow without real lights, which keeps the chamber at a fixed 7 lights so shader programs never recompile.
