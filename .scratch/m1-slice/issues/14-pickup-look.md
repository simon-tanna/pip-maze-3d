# 14: Gold pickups and the chest-open moment

Spec: [Milestone 1, the vertical slice](../spec.md)

**What to build:** Key, coin and chest become gold props that glow without real lights, and opening the chest becomes a moment. See the spec's _Render → Pickups_ and ADR 0001.

- One bpy pickups GLB with nodes `key`, `coin`, `chest` and `chest_lid`: `GOLD` / `GOLD_LIGHT` metal, subtle `GOLD_LIGHT` emissive that blooms, and an additive floor glow decal under each. No PointLights. The builder registers with `pnpm assets:build`.
- Key (~0.6 m) floats ~0.5 m up, bobs 0.12 m over 900 ms (sine yoyo) and turns once every 3 s. Coin (~0.4 m): the same bob, plus a turn with a seeded phase (render-local RNG). Chest (~1.2 m): emissive pulse over 900 ms (yoyo).
- Chest open (replacing ticket 06's placeholder): squash (1.2, 0.8) 80 ms → (0.9, 1.2) 140 ms → (1, 1) 120 ms; lid to ~110° over 220 ms; emissive burst; 160 ms shake.
- Key fade-in for reveal and respawn (ticket 07's `keyLost`): from scale 0.4 over 500 ms (Back).
- Under reduced motion: no bob or turn, no shake. Bob amplitude, turn rate and shake offset are readable through `look` or `sim` so e2e can assert them.
- Sizes and motion values live in the look module.

## Comments

- 2026-11-15: Agent build on `ticket/14-pickup-look`. `blender/scripts/build_pickups.py` (registered with `pnpm assets:build`) writes `public/models/pickups.glb`: `key` (bow ring, collar, shaft, two teeth; 0.6 m), `coin` (rimmed disc with a boss; 0.4 m), `chest` (1.2 m bevelled body, base trim, straps, lock plate) and `chest_lid` (slab and flattened half-barrel with straps and hasp, modelled from its hinge). Two Principled materials, `pickup_gold` (key, coin) and `chest_gold` (chest, lid), both `GOLD` metal with a `GOLD_LIGHT` emission; the view sets emissive intensity from `look.pickups` each frame, so the chest's pulse and burst never touch the keys. The contract (node names, sizes, `LID_HINGE`) lives in `src/render/pickups.ts` and `tests/unit/pickups-glb.test.ts` checks it, plus no lights in the GLB. The view (`createPickupsView(gltf, { reducedMotion })`) floats keys and coins at `floatHeight`, bobs them 0.12 m over 900 ms (sine yoyo), turns the key once per 3 s and each coin from a `mulberry32` phase seeded by the level seed, leaning both back toward the chase camera (`tiltDeg` 55; from the ~66° camera, upright pieces read as slivers) and spinning them about the leaned axis. The chest pulses over 900 ms; `openChest(at)` plays squash (1.2, 0.8) 80 ms → (0.9, 1.2) 140 ms → (1, 1) 120 ms, the lid to 110° over 220 ms (cubic-out), an emissive burst (and floor-glow boost) fading over 450 ms, and a 160 ms shake that `camera-rig.ts` adds along the camera's own x/y (new optional 4th `update` argument; `cameraRig.shake` is readable). `keyRevealed`/`keyLost` grow the key from 0.4 over 500 ms (Back-out). Every pickup has an additive `GOLD_LIGHT` floor-glow decal (a radial `DataTexture`, shared materials). Everything runs on simulation time, so `pause`/`advance` replay it. Under reduced motion: no bob, turn or shake; the squash, lid and fade still play. Observables: `__game.pickupsView.motion()` (`{ bobAmplitude, turnRate }`) and `__game.cameraRig.shake`. All sizes and motion numbers are in `look.pickups`. `@assets/models` gained `gltfNodes(gltf)` (what `loadGltf` now wraps) so unit tests build the view from the real GLB. `tests/e2e/pickup-look.spec.ts`: the key fades back in on its tile after a flame death, with the scene's light count and rig-guard violations unchanged; bob, turn, lid and shake under normal motion; all zero under reduced motion. **Precompile fix (shared, in `main.ts`):** the director's precompile now compiles against the composer's read buffer (linear, no tone mapping, which is how the RenderPass draws) instead of the canvas.
