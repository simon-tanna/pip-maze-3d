# 18: Moss and vines you can see (subtask of 16)

Spec: [Milestone 1, the vertical slice](../spec.md)
Parent: [16: Decor](16-decor.md)

**What to build:** Moss and vines visibly dress the chamber walls. This is a requirement: ticket 16 shipped moss that the human can't see in play, and dropped vines because v2's `DECOR_KINDS` has none.

- **Moss is missing in play.** `planDecor` places it: seeds 42, 1, 7, 123 and 999 give 2–6 moss per level for levels 0 and 1. So the loss is in the asset or the view (`blender/scripts/build_decor.py`, `src/render/decor.ts`): the patch's orientation after the Z-up → Y-up export, its depth relative to the kit's south face (`chamber-kit.ts`: the face sits inside the wall tile; only the cap lip passes the edge), its scale, or its contrast against sandstone from the chase camera. Find the cause first (use the `diagnosing-bugs` skill). Then fix it so moss reads clearly on the south face of the wall to the north, the face the north-chase camera sees.
- **Vines are new (not a v2 port).** Hanging vines on south-facing wall faces, growing down from the cap. They are bpy-procedural props in `decor.glb`, using only palette colours (`MOSS` / `MOSS_DARK`, or a new palette key if none fits; ADR 0001's reservations still apply). They are instanced from the plan like the other kinds.
- **v2 parity stays.** v2's tiles and kinds for bones, rubble, moss and pot still match `tests/fixtures/decor-baseline.json`. Vines must not change v2's first two draws per tile. Plan them from later draws of the same per-tile `tileRng` stream, or from a stream of their own, so they can share a tile with moss, or have tiles to themselves on any wall-north floor tile. Keep the 16 rules: never on a hazard, pickup, the critical path or the spawn zone, and the generator stream is untouched.
- Placement numbers (vine length and density, moss scale and offset) go in `look.decor`.

## Comments

- 2026-11-15: Agent build on `ticket/18-moss-and-vines`. **Moss root cause:** not orientation, depth or the plan. On level 0 seed 42 the moss instances are present (4), their world bounds sit 1–14 cm in front of the face of the wall to the north, and a ray from the chase camera to each patch's centre hits the moss before the face. It was invisible because it was too small and too dark: a sine-tapered mound at most 0.74 m tall (about 0.4 of its 1.2 × 0.8 m box) at the foot of a shadowed south face, which the steep chase camera (15.7 m up, 7 m back) turned into ~120 pixels at 640 × 400, in `MOSS` (`#45583a`) that the warm torch and fill lit to within ~17 levels of RGB of the face beside it. Showing the patch red-emissive found it at once; its true colour couldn't be told from the face. Nothing checked visibility: the tests checked instance translations only. **Fix:** a new palette key `MOSS_LIGHT` (`#9dcc5a`, for moss and vines; `MOSS` measured < 80 changed pixels even at the new size) and a bigger, fuller patch (`MOSS_WIDTH` 1.6 m, `MOSS_HEIGHT` 1.3 m, high at its ends too), with `look.decor.mossScale` and `mossOffset` (its slide is clamped so it stays on its wall). **Vines:** `planVines(level, seed, chance)` in `src/core/decor.ts`, on any plain floor tile with a wall to the north outside `avoidTiles`, from a stream of their own (`tileRng(seed, col + 1500, row + 1500)`), so `planDecor` and v2's baseline are untouched and a vine can share a tile with moss or any prop, or have one alone. `decor_vine` in `decor.glb`: five strands of leaves hanging from the cap edge, held 7 cm off the face so they clear the cap lip. The view hangs them at `WALL_HEIGHT`, scales their length to `look.decor.vineLength` less up to `vineJitter` of it, and `look.decor.vineChance` (default `VINE_CHANCE` 0.18) is read when a level is built. **Tests:** `decor.test.ts` (vines: same seed, same vines; north-wall plain floor only, never avoided; their own stream; density; present on levels 0 and 1 at seed 42), `decor-glb.test.ts` (the vine node's frame and lip clearance, the moss's size and south-facing area, `MOSS_LIGHT`), `decor-view.test.ts` (vine and moss matrices, no shadows from either), and `tests/e2e/decor.spec.ts`, which now also frames the chase camera as if the hero stood on the first moss and vine tile and counts the pixels that change when the kind is hidden: at least 150 at 640 × 400 (moss now ~370–400, vines ~180–380; ticket 16's moss would fail).
